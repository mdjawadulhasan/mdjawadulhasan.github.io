Current research topic✳

Visual AI assistants for scientific data: capability, a proof of concept, and a layer of healthy skepticism.

I’m building a healthy skepticism layer for LLM-based agentic systems that shows where an answer comes from and how it was produced.

The aim is to distinguish results computed from source data from model-generated inferences, and make supporting evidence visible. This should help users assess answers, identify potentially hallucinated claims, and decide when to trust the system.
